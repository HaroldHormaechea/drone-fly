# Use Case 28: Heatmap on real coordinates with complete, body-schematic neuron coverage

## Summary
The web-record heatmap (MRI-style full-brain activation heatmap, UC-12) and the dependency-free playback viewer (UC-05/06/15, `viz/viewer.js`) render neuron activations at 3-D positions. UC-27 makes real anatomically-accurate soma coordinates the tokenless default (`somaLocation` from the connectome meta CSV → `<stem>_soma.csv` / `<stem>_positions.csv`). This UC makes the heatmap **render on those real coordinates** and guarantees **complete coverage**: every neuron in the connectome appears, with none silently dropped. Neurons with a real soma (central neurons: visual projection, descending/motor, interneurons) render at their true anatomical position and are the visually dominant "brain". Neurons that are genuinely soma-less in the brain volume — real peripheral sensory afferents whose cell bodies sit outside the imaged brain (they have no `somaLocation`) — are placed in a **schematic fly body around the brain**, grouped by their real sensory-origin label (`cell_type`/`superclass`): `leg`, `haltere`, `campaniform`/wing-associated, VNC/ascending sensory, and a neutral torso/thorax group for generic/blank labels. Placement is deterministic and honest (no fabricated precise xyz — it uses the real categorical body-region label to position a schematic cluster). A **brain-scale guardrail** keeps the real brain visually dominant: body-region clusters are offset by multiples of the brain's own bounding box, capped so the schematic body never dwarfs the brain. The randomly-selected modality/observation-function neurons (UC-13 populations) are real connectome neurons and mostly render at real somas; the viewer can optionally color/tag them by modality. Scope is visualization + coordinate-completeness only: no change to training dynamics, observation schema, connectome graph, reward, or checkpoints; the viewer stays dependency-free; hermetic CI stays green and offline. Sequenced to build on top of UC-27.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Renders on real coordinates:** given a coordinate set with real `somaLocation`-derived positions, the heatmap/viewer places each real-soma neuron at its true anatomical position (verified against known fixture coordinates).
2. **Complete coverage — no neuron dropped:** the number of neurons rendered equals the connectome's neuron count; every `bodyid` has a rendered point, including soma-less afferents and modality-function neurons. A test asserts rendered-count == neuron-count (zero omissions).
3. **Body-region schematic placement (deterministic):** soma-less afferents are positioned in body-region clusters derived from their `cell_type`/`superclass` label — `leg`, `haltere`, `campaniform`/wing, VNC/ascending — at anatomically-plausible offsets relative to the brain (legs below/around the thorax, haltere/wing at the thorax, etc.). Same neuron → same position (reproducible). A test asserts afferents of a given region label land in that region's cluster.
4. **Generic/unknown → torso:** afferents with a blank or unrecognized region label are placed in a neutral torso/thorax group — separated from the brain, never misassigned to a specific limb. A test asserts an unlabeled afferent lands in the torso group, not a leg/haltere cluster.
5. **Brain-scale guardrail:** the real brain remains the visually dominant element; body-region clusters are offset by a bounded multiple of the brain's bounding-box size (schematic, compressed — not true fly proportions), so adding the body does not shrink the brain below a documented fraction of the view. A test asserts the brain bbox stays ≥ a defined fraction of the total rendered extent.
6. **Visual distinction:** schematic-placed (body) neurons are rendered distinguishably from real-anatomy (brain) neurons (e.g. glyph/opacity), in addition to being spatially separated, so a schematic dot is never mistaken for a real soma. Activation coloring still applies to both.
7. **Modality-function tagging (toggle):** the viewer can color/tag the UC-13 modality populations (vision/proprioception/hunger/damage) by function, as a toggle that does not override the default activation coloring.
8. **Graceful handling / alignment:** a coordinate set mixing real, schematic, and missing entries renders without crashing, without NaN/undefined points, and without misaligning activation values to the wrong neuron (the bodyid join stays exact).
9. **Scope containment + CI:** no change to training/obs/connectome/reward/checkpoints; the viewer stays dependency-free; `node --check viz/viewer.js` passes; full suite (`ruff check .`, `ruff format --check .`, `uv run pytest`) green and offline. Placement constants (region offsets, brain-scale cap) and the region-label mapping are named/documented.

## Potential Pitfalls & Open Questions
- **Dependency on UC-27** — requires UC-27's real-coordinate provisioning; this UC is sequenced to implement AFTER UC-27 merges and builds on `<stem>_soma.csv`/`<stem>_positions.csv` + the per-neuron `has_position`/`source` fields.
- **Edge case (label coverage)** — some afferents may carry a blank/generic `cell_type`; those fall to the torso group (AC-4), not a specific limb. Region-label → cluster mapping must be documented and easily extended as labels appear.
- **Edge case (activation↔neuron alignment)** — completeness must not misalign activations to positions; the bodyid join must stay exact (AC-8).
- **Assumption** — schematic body placement uses only the real categorical body-region label; the exact cluster *position* is a documented schematic choice (no real peripheral xyz exists in the dataset), so it is honestly labeled as schematic, distinct from real brain anatomy.
- **Assumption** — "the heatmap"/viewer is the web record renderer (`viz/viewer.js`) and/or the MRI heatmap generator; the dev-team pins the exact surface(s) that consume the coordinate file.

## Clarifications
- Q: Are the modality/observation-function neurons real or virtual?
  A: **Real** connectome neurons, selected by real biological labels (`modality.select_modality`: `visual_projection`, `mechanosensory_proprioceptive`, hunger/feeding, etc.). Never synthetic. Most have real somas → render at true anatomy.
- Q: Do we know where the soma-less afferents' cell bodies are on the body?
  A: No real 3-D coordinate exists (that's why `somaLocation` is empty), but we DO have a real **categorical body-region label** per afferent via `cell_type`/`superclass` (`leg`, `haltere`, `campaniform` sensilla, `vnc_sensory`, `sensory_ascending`).
- Q: How should soma-less afferents be placed in the heatmap?
  A: In a **schematic fly body around the brain**, grouped by that body-region label at anatomically-plausible offsets relative to the brain; generic/blank labels → a neutral torso/thorax group (separated from the brain, not assigned to a specific limb). Deterministic placement.
- Q: How to avoid the brain being dwarfed?
  A: **Brain-scale guardrail** — body clusters offset by a bounded multiple of the brain bbox (schematic/compressed, not true fly proportions); the brain stays the dominant element above a documented fraction of the view.
- Q: Visual distinction and function tagging?
  A: Schematic-placed neurons rendered distinctly from real-anatomy neurons (glyph/opacity + spatial separation); modality-function populations can be color/tagged by function via a viewer toggle that doesn't override activation coloring.
- Q: Does this change training/obs/checkpoints?
  A: No — visualization + coordinate-completeness only; viewer stays dependency-free; hermetic CI green + offline. Builds on and is sequenced after UC-27.
