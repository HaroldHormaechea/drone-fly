# Use Case 48: Make pybullet dynamics randomization thrust-to-weight preserving

## Summary
Fix the mass/thrust-scale mismatch that UC-47 proved is the root cause of the drone never taking off. On the pybullet backend, domain randomization samples an *absolute* body mass (~1 kg, sized for the SimpleDroneAdapter point-mass model where `BASE_MAX_THRUST = 2·m·g`) and `PyBulletAdapter.reconfigure` applies it to the CF2X body (native 0.027 kg, motor thrust `KF` sized for 0.027 kg) **while ignoring `max_thrust`**, yielding thrust-to-weight ≈ 0.24 → free-fall at any throttle. This use case makes dynamics randomization **T/W-preserving on pybullet** so a randomized-heavier drone still has enough thrust to fly — matching the simple backend, whose T/W is already preserved by construction. Preferred approach (A): scale the motor thrust scale (`KF` / `max_thrust` and the `hover_rpm`/`max_rpm` the `ctbr_to_rpm` mixer derives) proportionally with the applied mass so peak T/W stays in the sane ~2 band, and interpret the randomized mass as CF2X-relative rather than an unphysical absolute ~1 kg. Fallback (B), only if (A) is too invasive for a minimal fix: gate dynamics randomization off on the pybullet backend behind a config flag until its parameterization is CF2X-consistent. This is a behavioral fix that intentionally changes training dynamics (UC-47 was diagnostic-only and deferred it); it touches only the pybullet dynamics/thrust path and leaves the reward, UC-44/UC-46 curricula, climb-bias init, and the mixer structure untouched.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. On the pybullet backend, applying a randomized heavier-than-native mass **preserves T/W**: the UC-47 harness baseline sweep at a randomized mass shows hover near throttle 0.5 (T/W ≈ 1.0) and climb at throttle 1.0 (T/W in a healthy ~1.5–2.5 band), NOT the 0.20–0.24 collapse. (Reported via the harness; real-pybullet, non-gating.)
2. The thrust scale actually used by the mixer (`KF` / `max_thrust` / `hover_rpm` / `max_rpm`) scales with applied mass such that computed **peak T/W stays within a target band (~1.8–2.2) across the entire randomized mass range** — expressible as a pure computation independent of the pybullet simulator.
3. Hermetic, CI-gating unit tests encode the T/W-preservation property on the reconfigure/thrust-setup **logic** without importing pybullet (assert the computed thrust scale vs applied mass, across the mass range).
4. The simple backend's dynamics are unchanged — its existing behavior and tests still pass (no regression to the already-correct path).
5. Diagnostic-adjacent invariant: no change to the reward function, the UC-44 airborne curriculum, the UC-46 attitude-authority curriculum, the climb-bias init, or the `ctbr_to_rpm` mixer structure (only its mass-dependent thrust-scale constants may change). The diff is confined to the pybullet dynamics/thrust path, the dynamics-randomization sampler/config, tests, and docs.
6. The new behavior is behind an additive, backward-compatible config flag (default: T/W-preserving ON) and is documented (README / relevant docs) where it changes a training-behavior contract.
7. A real-pybullet re-run via the UC-47 harness confirms the fix (reported in the PR, non-gating): a randomized-mass drone can hover/climb, and the episode_350 recorded mass no longer forces free-fall.
8. Full CI gate green: `ruff check .`, `ruff format --check .`, and `pytest` all pass (mypy is not a CI step).
9. If approach (B) is chosen instead of (A): dynamics randomization is gated off on pybullet behind a documented flag, and the same T/W-sanity assertion holds (native-mass CF2X remains flyable, no ~1 kg body applied).

## Potential Pitfalls & Open Questions
- **Ambiguity** — Approach (A) preserve-T/W vs (B) gate-off. Analyst/challenger decide against the actual code; (A) preferred if the `KF`/`hover_rpm`/`max_rpm` plumbing is tractable. Either way AC1–AC3 (T/W preservation / sanity) is the contract.
- **Assumption** — The randomized mass likely needs to be interpreted as a **CF2X-relative multiplier** of native 0.027 kg on pybullet, not an absolute ~1 kg (a 1 kg CF2X quad is physically wrong). The sampler/config may itself need a backend-aware interpretation; the simple backend keeps its absolute-mass semantics.
- **Edge case** — pybullet inertia: `changeDynamics(mass=…)` without a consistent inertia-tensor update can desync mass and rotational inertia. Decide whether inertia must scale with mass too (physical consistency) and document the choice.
- **Edge case / Assumption** — Drag randomization: whether `drag` also needs CF2X-relative treatment. Primary scope is mass/thrust; note and handle drag only if it materially affects T/W or stability.
- **Risk** — This changes the training-dynamics distribution for all future pybullet runs. That is intended (the current distribution is degenerate/unflyable), but it invalidates checkpoints and recordings made on the broken scale — a **fresh** GPU retrain is required to see takeoff (as with UC-44/46, fresh matters).
- **Assumption** — Real-pybullet validation is non-gating (CI is hermetic — no pybullet); the CI-gating proof is the pure thrust-scale computation (AC2/AC3).
- **Missing input** — Exact `KF` / `hover_rpm` / `max_rpm` derivation and where mass/thrust are plumbed: analyst reads `src/drone_fly/adapter/pybullet_adapter.py` and the dynamics-randomization sampler to pin the precise change points.
