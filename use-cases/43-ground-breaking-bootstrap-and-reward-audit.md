# Use Case 43: Sub-threshold ground-breaking reward to bootstrap takeoff + reward-system audit

## Summary
UC-40/41/42 removed the freeze, the crash-cliff, and the perverse once-airborne gradient — but a fresh full-stack run (~99k steps) shows the drone **never breaks ground at all**. Across every sampled recording z is pinned at ~0.0135 m (resting height) — no hop, no lift-and-drop — and `ep_rew_mean` is glued to exactly −5 (pure time penalty), which proves the *entire* episode population is planted, not just the samples (any airborne time would add the airborne bonus and lift the mean). The clipped throttle mean sits flat at hover (~0.48) and is **not rising** over training. Root chicken-and-egg: the airborne survival flag only trips at `z > floor_z + floor_epsilon` (≈0.05 m; `racing_env.py:384`), so UC-42's graded airborne reward — and any airborne credit — **never activates** while the drone rests at 0.0135 m; and there is no reward gradient strong enough *below* that threshold to pull it up, so PPO gets no signal favouring higher throttle → the throttle mean never rises → it never gets airborne. Breaking ground needs *sustained* consecutive above-hover throttle, which per-step exploration noise around a 0.48 mean effectively never produces. This use case (a) implements a **non-farmable sub-threshold "ground-breaking" reward** that provides a positive gradient for upward progress from the resting height toward the airborne threshold, so PPO is rewarded for the first few centimetres of lift *before* the airborne bonus can engage; and (b) performs a **reward-system audit** of every term for interactions/conflicts that suppress takeoff or create perverse incentives, fixing the clearly-wrong ones (with evidence, invariant-preserving) and documenting the rest. **Governing design principle (user directive):** *any* nudge in the right direction toward *any* desired outcome — no matter how small — MUST be rewarded. There must be no threshold/gating "dead zone" where genuine incremental progress toward a goal (breaking ground, gaining altitude toward target, reducing distance to the next gate, passing a gate, etc.) earns zero or negative signal. The airborne-flag gate at ~0.05 m is the canonical offender the bootstrap reward fixes, but the audit must hunt for *every* such dead zone. Dense crediting stays **non-farmable** (potential-based/telescoping — a step forward pays, a round-trip nets ≈0). It touches `src/drone_fly/env/reward.py`, `src/drone_fly/env/config.py` (RewardConfig), possibly `src/drone_fly/env/racing_env.py` (only if the audit finds a genuine gating/assembly bug), and `README.md`.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Diagnosis with evidence** of why no current reward gradient bootstraps ground-breaking: quantify the sub-threshold behaviour of the existing climb potential (does `Φ=w·min(h,target)` reward h∈[z_rest, threshold], and does it telescope to ~0 for the tiny sub-threshold movements a resting drone makes?), the airborne-flag gating (`racing_env.py:384`), and why the throttle mean has no gradient to rise while no episode gets airborne. Confirm from the recordings/`ep_rew_mean=−5` that the population is planted.
2. **Sub-threshold ground-breaking reward implemented**, demonstrated by a deterministic reward-math test: a trajectory that rises from the resting height toward the airborne threshold earns **strictly increasing** return with height *below* the threshold (i.e. there is now a positive gradient in [z_rest, threshold]), and staying planted earns strictly less. The reward must be **non-farmable**: a bob (up then back down) nets ≈0 (potential-based/telescoping), and there is no reward for hovering-in-place at the floor. It must connect **continuously** to UC-42's graded airborne reward at the threshold (no discontinuity, no double-count).
3. **Reward-system audit**: every reward term is reviewed for takeoff-suppressing interactions or perverse incentives — `time_penalty`, `progress` (distance-to-gate), climb potential, airborne survival (UC-42 graded), collision penalty + UC-41 curriculum, gate/completion bonuses, obstacle penalty, anti-suicide, and the stuck/grounded early-termination. The audit is written up (findings + verdict per term). Any clearly-wrong, takeoff-blocking issue is fixed **with evidence** and invariant-preservation; anything ambiguous or out-of-scope is documented as a follow-up rather than changed speculatively (no scope-creep rewrites).
4. **All prior invariants preserved and no regression**: UC-37/38/39 (potential-based/telescoping, non-farmable, ≈0 on floor, capped at target, per-episode shaping < completion_bonus, climb_gamma==gamma, penalty-free stuck-cut, anti-suicide), UC-40 hover-bias, UC-41 hold-then-ramp collision curriculum, UC-42 graded airborne reward. Existing reward/health tests stay green (adjusted only where the contract legitimately changes).
5. **VecNormalize-aware validation**: reward-math tests gate on **relative/structural** properties (gradient sign/monotonicity below threshold, non-farmable bob, continuity at threshold, explicit "unchanged terms" assertions), never an absolute margin (reward VecNormalize makes global rescales a no-op). The numpy `simple`-adapter smoke-train stays a wires/finite/no-collapse check only. A real-pybullet behavioral probe (orchestrator-run, non-committed) may confirm that a scripted gentle climb now crosses the threshold and accrues the bootstrap reward — supporting evidence only, not the gate; behavioral takeoff-learning remains the user's GPU retrain.
6. If any RewardConfig value changes, the README reward table + bound prose sync and `test_reward_docs.py`/`test_bootstrap_docs.py`/`test_viz_contract.py` stay green. Full suite green: `uv run --extra dev pytest`.
7. **Dense progress crediting — no dead zones (user directive):** the audit explicitly enumerates every *desired outcome* (break ground, climb toward target altitude, reduce distance to the next required gate, pass a gate, complete the course) and confirms that for each there is a **dense, non-farmable gradient that credits any incremental progress** — no threshold/gate/clip where real progress toward that outcome earns zero or goes negative. Each identified dead zone is either fixed (with a deterministic reward-math test proving a small step toward the outcome now yields strictly-positive shaping, and a bob/round-trip nets ≈0) or explicitly documented with rationale if genuinely out of scope. The airborne-flag gate is one instance; the criterion is general.

## Potential Pitfalls & Open Questions
- **Risk (non-farmability):** a naive "reward any upward Δz below threshold" is farmable by bobbing. The sub-threshold term must be potential-based (telescoping) or otherwise provably non-farmable — a bob nets ≈0.
- **Risk (continuity/double-count at the threshold):** the sub-threshold reward and UC-42's graded airborne reward must join smoothly at `floor_z+floor_epsilon` without a jump or overlap that could be farmed at the boundary, and the combined per-episode total must still stay < `completion_bonus`.
- **Assumption / open question:** reward shaping alone may be *necessary but not sufficient* — the deeper blocker is that random per-step exploration around hover rarely produces the *sustained* consecutive above-hover needed to break contact. A stronger sub-threshold gradient increases the payoff for any exploratory lift and should help PPO reinforce it, but if evidence shows exploration itself can't produce the sustained push, temporally-correlated exploration or a higher initial takeoff-bias (fresh-build) is the documented follow-up (out of scope here unless the audit shows it's the only viable lever).
- **Edge case:** the resting height (~0.0135 m) vs `floor_z`/`floor_epsilon` exact values must be read from code so the sub-threshold band [z_rest, threshold] is defined correctly; the reward must be ~0 exactly at rest (non-farmable floor).
- **Risk (audit scope-creep):** the reward audit could balloon; keep fixes to clearly-wrong takeoff-blockers with evidence, document the rest.
