# Use Case 64: Calibrate and connect the live harness to real Liftoff (Arena / Infinity Loop, micro drone)

## Summary
Take the UC62 live harness from "tested against fakes and recorded CSVs" to "running against a live Liftoff session", using **Liftoff Arena, race "04 - Infinity Loop"** with the **micro "Test small drone"** (FT Gremlin frame, FX805 micro cam, lens "None", ZetaFlight Acro, Betaflight rates 100/0/70) as the reference setup. A hand-flown capture (`artifacts/harness/captures/2026-10-11-infinity-manual`: raw UDP datagrams + 2,043 wall-clock-stamped 2560×1080 frames, 182 s of flight, saved replay "04 - Infinity Loop - 00_00_000 - 2026-10-11 - AI training") already established that the packaged config has **roll and pitch gyro signs inverted**, that MotorRPM carries a uint8 count prefix (97-byte datagrams), that the micro hovers at **−0.06** throttle input, and that **120° HFOV / 20° uptilt** is correct (XML arches projected through telemetry pose land within a few pixels on the frames). Track/race XMLs and prop collider shapes were extracted with `liftoff-flight-analyzer` (`tracks`, `props`) into `artifacts/harness/trackdata/`; the per-machine calibration overlay is `artifacts/harness/liftoff-arena-infinity.yaml`. This use case packages the calibration as a preset; replaces red-quad-only gate detection with a **multi-type learned gate detector trained on frames auto-labelled by projecting XML/props gate geometry through the telemetry pose** (so new gate styles — up to ~3 per course — need a capture + retrain, not new code); parses real linked-list race routes; masks HUD/ghost-list/racing-line regions in code; adds a pad stick-direction check and a focus fail-safe; calibrates the depth model so it can supply the 40-ray raycast; and ends with a **staged live run — 30-dim model first, then the 70-dim obstacle model** with live depth → raycast — both judged on I/O and fail-safe, the 70-dim run being the acceptance target. Training code, `gpu_prototype/` and the inference contract stay read-only, as in UC62.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **Packaged preset:** a micro/Arena `liftoff` preset ships in `src/drone_fly/harness/configs/` with corrected `gyro_map` (wx = −roll, wy = +pitch, wz = −yaw), `gyro_units: deg_s`, `rpm_count_prefix: true`, `hover_stick: -0.06`, `hfov_deg: 120`, `uptilt_deg: 20`, `resolution: [2560, 1080]`, world scale, the fitted depth calibration (AC9), HUD masks (AC7), and a **per-gate-type table** (`itemID` pattern → class, aperture and outer dimensions; Infinity Loop: `InflatableArchBrandless01`, aperture 6.18 × 3.69 m, outer 8.0 × 4.46 m, open at the bottom). Every value carries a comment naming its measurement source. The 5-inch default receives the same gyro/RPM fixes (they describe Liftoff, not the airframe).
2. **Telemetry regression:** a committed fixture of a few seconds of real 97-byte datagrams passes `gyro_check` (`ok=True`, |corr| ≥ 0.95, |scale| within 1 ± 0.05) under the preset and fails under the old signs.
3. **Race route parsing:** the offline parser follows `checkPointPassages` from the `Start` passage through `nextPassageIDs` to `Finish`, resolves `checkPointID` to track instances, repeats per `requiredLaps`, and supports checkpoints visited more than once (Infinity Loop passes the centre arch twice per lap). Tested on a synthetic fixture with that structure; the existing flat-list format keeps working.
4. **Auto-labelling tool:** given a capture plus its track/race XML and props, projects every gate of every configured type into each frame and writes per-frame labels (class, image keypoints of the gate outline, visibility, range); frames inside respawn/discontinuity segments are dropped. Uses the Unity → camera conventions verified in this session (pinhole, `R_x(−uptilt)`, wall-clock pairing of frames to telemetry). The projection maths is tested on synthetic geometry.
5. **Learned gate estimator:** a new estimator behind `GateEstimator` (lazy-imported backend, selected by config, registered without runner edits) detects gates of any configured type and derives `rel`, `curr_gate`, `next_lookahead` from detected keypoints plus that type's known dimensions (PnP), keeping the existing lost-gate / acquire / pass semantics. Class → dimensions comes from the AC1 gate-type table. Ships a training entry point that consumes AC4 labels.
6. **Offline gate scoring:** `drone-fly harness eval` on the Infinity Loop capture, using a held-out time split not used for training, reports per gate type, for gates 4–25 m ahead: detection rate, median position error, median yaw error, and per-frame inference latency on the local GPU. Targets: ≥ 80 % detection, median position error ≤ 15 % of range.
7. **Frame masking:** a configurable list of screen-region masks (OSD, stick widgets, lap / ghost list) is applied before gate detection, auto-labelling and depth sampling; the preset ships masks for the Arena HUD at 2560×1080. A test shows content inside a mask yields no detection and no depth hit.
8. **Pad stick-direction check:** a `drone-fly harness` subcommand sends small known stick pulses through `vgamepad` while reading live telemetry, reports per axis the resulting body-rate sign and approximate magnitude versus the inverse rate curve, prints suggested `stick_signs`, and goes neutral on F12, stale telemetry, or loss of game focus. Unit-tested with fakes.
9. **Depth calibration and raycast scoring:** an offline step fits `depth.calibration.scale/shift` on the capture against XML-derived geometry (arches, barriers, flags), reports the fit error, and scores the reconstructed 40-ray scan against the contract's `raycast()` ground truth on the same frames (median ray error, share of rays within 0.5 m at training scale). The fitted values go into the preset.
10. **Focus fail-safe:** the runner fails safe (neutral rates + throttle cut) when the Liftoff window loses foreground focus (pad input otherwise reaches whichever window is focused). Tested with a fake focus probe.
11. **Staged live run:** `drone-fly harness run --harness liftoff` with the preset runs live on Infinity Loop in two stages — (a) the **30-dim** model with the learned gate estimator and `free_space_estimator: none`, then (b) the **70-dim** obstacle model with the `depth` free-space estimator. Each stage sustains 50 Hz (or logs missed deadlines), logs perception estimate age, writes a complete per-run folder under `artifacts/`, and fails safe cleanly on F12, stale telemetry, gate loss, focus loss and — for (b) — stale depth estimates. Stage (b) passing is the acceptance target; flight success is not required.
12. **GPU budget:** a measured per-tick latency breakdown (state, gate detector, depth, policy, actuation) for stage (b) on the RTX 3050 with Liftoff rendering, recorded in the run folder and the ADR.
13. **Tests and data:** CI uses only committed fixtures (trimmed real telemetry bytes, synthetic XML/images). Real-capture tests (labelling, eval, depth fit) are local opt-in and skip when the data is absent. Track XMLs, props tables and game frames are never committed.
14. **Docs:** an ADR records the real-Liftoff findings (inverted gyro signs, RPM count prefix, race-route format, multi-type gates via auto-labelling, HUD masking, pad focus behaviour, GPU budget) and the "add a gate style = capture → label → retrain" workflow, including how to extract track data with `liftoff-flight-analyzer`.
15. Existing tests stay green; ruff passes; new deps go only into the `harness` extra; nothing under `training/`, `gpu_prototype/`, or `docs/INFERENCE_CONTRACT.md` changes.

## Potential Pitfalls & Open Questions
- **Risk** — Training-data volume: one 182 s capture gives ~1,600 in-flight frames (~380 with an arch in range), one gate type, one track — likely too little / too correlated to generalise. Expect to request 2–3 more hand-flown captures (including other tracks) before AC6 holds.
- **Risk** — GPU latency: gate detector + depth model + policy on one RTX 3050 while Liftoff renders may not hold 50 Hz in stage (b); depth already runs async with an age limit, and model size may need to drop.
- **Missing input** — Detector architecture (keypoint model vs segmentation + outline fit; which pretrained backbone) — left to the analyst/challenger within the latency budget.
- **Missing input** — Which 70-dim checkpoint: several exist (`training/11-count-curriculum`, the newer exp11 varied-shapes model). Default: the newest checkpoint with `extra_dim` 70, chosen at run time.
- **Assumption** — AC6 thresholds (80 % detection, 15 % of range) are first guesses and may be revised once first scores exist.
- **Risk** — Rate gap: models are capped at ~230 °/s vs the pilot's ~2,600 °/s and 20 m/s; with world_scale ~4 the policy may be very timid or not cope. Acceptable under AC11's I/O-only criteria.
- **Edge case** — AI ghost drones are moving objects that masking cannot remove; in stage (b) depth may read them as obstacles. Prefer flying the live run without ghosts if Liftoff allows it.
- **Edge case** — HUD masks are layout-specific (2560×1080, Arena HUD); other resolutions/HUD configs need their own masks.
- **Risk** — Concurrent work: another agent changes the training regime on `main`; implementation happens on a separate branch/worktree, and the 70-dim checkpoint may change underneath the run.
